# A Knight's Burden

_Every step has a cost._

## Synopsis

In *A Knight's Burden*, you are a loyal knight sworn to serve your king. But a creeping sickness expands within you—a fatal cancer that drains your life with every exertion. When the king demands you retrieve a sacred diamond from a perilous land, your sense of duty is pitted against your own mortality.

> This game was created in 9 days for the Godot game jam #85.

## Gameplay

The core of the game is a constant, tense balance between action and self-preservation.

* **Manage Your Burden:** Every action—running, dashing, or fighting—drains your energy and causes the cancer within you to **expand** faster. This is visualized on screen as a creeping, darkening overlay.
* **Seek Relief:** Explore the world to find and complete objectives. Each success grants you a temporary "cure," pushing the disease back and giving you precious more time to complete your quest.
* **A Race Against Time:** You must find the sacred diamond and fulfill your duty before the sickness consumes you entirely. Choose your path wisely, as every moment counts.

## Theme: Expansion

The game interprets the theme of **Expansion** in two key ways:

1.  **Literal Expansion:** The core conflict is the physical cancer spreading and expanding throughout the knight's body, represented both visually on the screen and mechanically through the ever-present threat of death.
2.  **Metaphorical Expansion:** With every step and every challenge, the knight's burden, the stakes of his quest, and the weight of his duty expand, pushing him to his absolute limit.
